Fig. 01 — Two pipelines, one platformevaluate / generate

AEvaluation

Teachers upload scanned handwritten answer sheets. Vision LLMs read each page, map every answer to its question, mark it against the answer key and marking scheme, and produce reports. A teacher reviews and finalises every mark; the model does the reading, the human keeps the decision.

BPaper generation

Board-pattern question papers built from NCERT textbooks using RAG over 13,000+ passages across 291 chapters. Marks and section rules are validated in code, not by the model, and an AI moderator loop flags wrong questions before anything is printed.

CEngineering details

  • Exam diagrams rendered as exact SVG from model-generated specs
  • Converter for legacy-font Hindi PDFs with 97.9% word agreement against a vision model, at no cost
  • Razorpay credit payments, and support for English, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam and Telugu
  • 800+ automated tests across pytest and Vitest
